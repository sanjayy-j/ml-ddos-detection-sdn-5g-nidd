# Detection scores under review

The original files in this study are retained unchanged. Their recorded
acceptance reflects the validation rules used at collection time.

A subsequent audit found multi-second telemetry backlog, incorrect time-window
assignment and incomplete readout in some trials. The earlier validation did
not check kernel-to-processing lag, independent sFlow reception or N=1 tail
completeness. Completion of the 54-trial matrix therefore does not establish
that its quality comparisons are reliable under the newer checks.

Do not substitute a favorable repeat, mix these quality values with new
measurements, or treat the repaired pilot as a replacement for all 54 trials.

See [the engineering investigation](../../../docs/experiments/collector-backlog-repair.md)
for diagnosis, repair, targeted verification and the separate full rerun path.
The manuscript has not been updated.
